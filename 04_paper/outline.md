# Φ-F/EQUITY Study: Cross-Domain Complexity Analysis

## Abstract
Comparative analysis of complexity (Φ) between biological (cardiac) and socio-economic systems.

## 1. Introduction
- Complexity theory across domains
- Φ as universal complexity metric
- Research question: Is the cardiac system more complex than economic systems?

## 2. Methodology
- Φ calculation from ECG signals (MIT-BIH)
- Φ calculation from economic data (WID/simulated)
- Statistical analysis framework

## 3. Results
- Cardiac Φ: 0.912 ± 0.060 (n=10)
- Economic Φ: 0.848 ± 0.040 (n=200)
- Statistical significance: p < 0.01
- Effect size: Cohen's d = 1.51

## 4. Discussion
- Implications for complexity theory
- Limitations and methodological considerations
- Future research directions

## 5. Conclusion
Cardiac systems demonstrate significantly higher complexity than economic systems.
