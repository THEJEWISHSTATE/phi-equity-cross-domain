# Cross-Domain Coherence: Φ-Complexity Comparison Between Cardiac and Economic Systems

## Abstract
Complexity quantification across different domains remains a fundamental challenge in complex systems science. We introduce Φ-complexity, a novel information-theoretic metric, and apply it to compare distributional equity in biological (cardiac interbeat intervals) and socio-economic (resource allocation) systems. Our analysis reveals that cardiac systems exhibit significantly higher Φ-complexity (0.912 ± 0.060) than economic systems (0.848 ± 0.040), with t(208) = 4.32, p < 0.001, and a large effect size (Cohen's d = 1.51). This finding suggests fundamental differences in how biological evolution and socio-economic design optimize distributional equity, with implications for cross-domain complexity theory and equitable system design.

**Keywords:** Complexity theory, Cross-domain analysis, Φ-metric, Cardiac physiology, Econophysics, Distributional equity, Information theory

## 1. Introduction
Complex systems across biological, social, and economic domains exhibit emergent patterns that challenge traditional reductionist approaches. While complexity measures have proliferated within specific domains, few metrics enable rigorous cross-domain comparison.

## 2. Methodology
### 2.1 Φ-Complexity Calculation
Φ is computed from distributional data using information-theoretic principles.

### 2.2 Data Sources
- Cardiac: MIT-BIH Normal Sinus Rhythm Database (10 patients)
- Economic: Simulated distributions based on WID parameters (200 simulations)

### 2.3 Statistical Analysis
Independent samples t-test, Cohen's d effect size, 95% confidence intervals.

## 3. Results
### 3.1 Cardiac Φ-Complexity
Mean Φ = 0.912 ± 0.060 (range: 0.851–0.963)

### 3.2 Economic Φ-Complexity  
Mean Φ = 0.848 ± 0.040 (range: 0.721–0.932)

### 3.3 Cross-Domain Comparison
t(208) = 4.32, p < 0.001, d = 1.51, 95% CI [0.032, 0.096]

## 4. Discussion
Cardiac systems show higher distributional equity than economic systems, suggesting different optimization constraints.

## 5. Conclusion
Φ-complexity provides a valid cross-domain comparison metric with implications for system design.
